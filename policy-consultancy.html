Policy & Consultancy

I do research consultancy and strategic advice for UK and international clients. Past clients have included the OECD, the European Investment Bank, the Asian Development Bank, and various UK government departments and charitable endowments such as Power to Change.

This work has included applied econometrics, strategic advisory work, evidence reviews, and policy-focused research projects.
